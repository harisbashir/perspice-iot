The **Overview** is the first page after signing in. It is a dashboard that summarises everything you can see.

## What it shows

- **Device counts** by status: how many devices are **online** (reporting as expected), **offline** (quiet for longer than their profile allows), **dead** (silent for a long time, a day by default) and **never connected**.
- **Messages in the last 24 hours**: how many values your devices sent.
- **Alarms**: the open alarms by severity, and the newest ones.
- **The device table**: every device you can see, with its status and when it was last heard from. Click a device to open it.

Everything updates live: when a device goes offline or an alarm is raised, the page changes without reloading.

## Time window and reports

Charts on the overview show the dashboard's **time window** (for example the last 24 hours). Use the time picker to look at another period; this only changes your own view.

**PDF report** creates a PDF of the page as it is now. It is prepared in the background; you get a notification when it is ready (see [Reports and exports](/help/reports-exports)).

:::platform
## For platform users

Platform users see every customer's devices, with a **customer** column and a customer filter in the table.

The overview is the dashboard template of kind **Overview**. Change it under **Dashboards** like any other template; every customer sees the change unless they have customised their copy. If no overview template exists the page says so, with a link to **Dashboards**.
:::
